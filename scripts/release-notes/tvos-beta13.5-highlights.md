## Highlights

A quick-turn hotfix built from u/mrStevenx3's same-day beta.13 review (thanks for the video — several of these were pinned frame-by-frame from it) plus one upstream port and two GitHub-issue fixes.

- **French (and every non-English) trailers come back** — beta.13's trailer-language fix widened the lookup so English fallbacks always exist, but the sorting could still pick an official English trailer over a French one ("72 Hours" and "Drop Game" from the review are the canonical cases). Your Metadata Language now wins outright when set: the auto-played trailer and the Trailers & Extras row both prefer your language first, with English and untagged videos as fallbacks only.
- **White-on-white in Appearance is fixed** — the focused toggle rows in Settings → Appearance (Accent Focus Ring, No Zoom on Focus) could wash out to a white pill with unreadable light text on real hardware. Settings rows now track their own focus state end-to-end, so the text reliably flips dark on the focus platter — and the theme swatches wear a focus ring in a shade that contrasts each swatch, White included.
- **Strip SDH Subtitles** (ported from Nuvio mobile, PR #1751) — a new toggle in Settings → Playback hides `[sound cues]`, `(parenthetical asides)` and `SPEAKER:` labels from text subtitles. Works in both players: mpv uses its native SDH filter; the AVPlayer path filters at WebVTT generation, embedded tracks and addon subtitle files alike.
- **Header-gated addon streams play now** — streams whose addon declares required HTTP headers (a Referer or User-Agent the CDN checks) silently spun forever, because the tvOS players never sent them (Nuvio iOS does — GitHub issue #2). Both players now pass the addon's sanitized headers, and credential-class headers are kept away from third-party subtitle hosts.
- **Square corners reach the whole detail page** — with Corners = Square, the season posters, trailer/extras thumbnails and episode stills all follow the setting now (they kept the default rounding before), focused and unfocused alike.
- **Season selector polish** — the "Episodes" heading now sits below the season posters where it belongs, it finally respects your app language ("Épisodes"!), and the Season 1 / Season 2 labels no longer hide under the poster's focus zoom. Trailer and episode captions got the same clearance.
- **Actor pages: "Known for" is localized** — the department label under an actor's name ("Acting") now translates. The biographies themselves already request your Metadata Language; when one still shows English it's because TMDB has no translation for that person (their own site shows the same English text).
- **Hero Paint Diagnostics** — Settings → About gains a diagnostics toggle for the "doubled hero artwork" report we still can't reproduce in-house: turn it on, relaunch, and a short log of the launch's hero paints renders right in the pane. If you see the doubled hero, a photo of that screen is exactly the evidence needed.
