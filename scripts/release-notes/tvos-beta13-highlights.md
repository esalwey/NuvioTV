## Highlights

- **The classic top info panel is back — drawn by Nuvio** — tvOS 26 removed the player's swipe-down panel, so this build draws its own: swipe down (or press down) in the native player for **Info · Subtitles · Audio** — poster, title, episode name, synopsis, a metadata strip (runtime · 4K · Dolby Vision · codec · audio · bitrate) and live stream details on the Info tab; the file's **embedded subtitle tracks** listed alongside your addon subtitles (forced/SDH flagged); and **every audio track** in the file with instant switching plus your current speakers/headphones and a route picker. Menu closes it; playback never pauses. The mpv player gets the **same panel** plus a fourth **Playback** tab (speed, subtitle/audio delay, episode jump list, alternate sources) — its old swipe-up menu is gone.
- **Home "Upcoming" row** — a new row under Continue Watching with the next airing episode of every show you follow: episode still, S02E05 badge, a TODAY / TOMORROW / IN N DAYS pill, soonest first. Continue Watching cards carry the same episode badge. Toggle it in Settings → Home Screen.
- **Self-hosted servers** — the Apple TV can now connect to your own Nuvio backend (NuvioMedia/self-host). Welcome screen → **Connect to a Server** (or Settings → Account & Services → **Connect to a Self-Hosted Server**): enter the backend URL, the app reads `/.well-known/nuvio`, shows the discovered backend, its sign-in methods and a trust warning (public host / plain HTTP), and connects after you confirm — that signs you out of the current server and clears local data on the Apple TV, like a sign-out. QR sign-in follows the server's `tv_login` capability (the approve page becomes `<backend>/tv-login`; servers without it get email & password as the primary sign-in). **Use Official Server** switches back. Same discovery protocol and trust UX as Nuvio's Android TV app. Tip: addresses without a scheme are treated as HTTPS — type `http://` explicitly for a plain-HTTP LAN server.
- **Edit TMDB Discover filters on the TV** — open a collection folder backed by a TMDB Discover (or company / network) source and press **Edit Filters**: sort, genres, keywords, studios, networks, watch providers and region, dates, ratings, language and country — including the **exclusion** filters that arrived on mobile (excluded genres / keywords / companies / watch providers) — with quick chips for the common IDs. Saves sync back to your account's collections, so the edit shows up on your phone too.
- **Trailers actually fill the tile now** — two fixes deep: focus-trailer zoom is measured per title, applied earlier, and **survives relaunch** — and the real reason zoomed trailers could still show bars is fixed (the zoom was computed but never rendered; the video layer now lives where the zoom can reach it). A reveal gate also keeps the artwork up until the crop decision is made from real frames, so a letterboxed first frame never flashes — and artwork with baked-in bars is detected and cropped too. If GIGN's neighbours still show bars for you, tell us.
- **Trailers stopped playing? That was the Metadata Language** — with Metadata Language set (e.g. Français), trailer lookups quietly failed for titles whose localized metadata lacked trailer entries. Trailers now fall back across languages, and changing the language no longer leaves a 20-minute stale window. Thanks u/mrStevenx3 for the repro that cracked it.
- **Theme picker labels are back** — on Appearance → Theme, the focused swatch's name went near-black on the dark background in beta.11/12 (the "colour picker has a black background" report). The name now brightens on focus on every theme, White included. Promised in beta.12's post — delivered.
- **Season posters** — the season selector on a show's page is now a row of 2:3 season art with the selected season outlined in your accent color (text chips remain for shows without season art).
- **Trailers on Focus keeps the title on screen** — with **Hide Titles** on, the playing tile carried no title at all. The title's logo (or its name when there's no logo art) now sits bottom-left on the tile over a soft scrim, still and while the trailer plays — the way Nuvio's own TV app does it. If your captions are visible under the cards, nothing changes (no double title).
- **Hide Discover** — Settings → Content Sources → Search Sources → **Hide Discover** hides the entire Discover section on the Search page (types, catalogs, genres and grid), leaving the search field and your recent searches. Synced per profile like the rest of your home-screen settings, and Search skips the Discover fan-out while it's hidden.
- **Card Depth "Top" you can actually see** — Edge Coverage → Top (*En haut*) drew a one-point hairline you couldn't make out from the couch, which is why "Full" looked right and "Top" looked broken. Top and Half now draw a lit-from-above rail over the top edge and corner shoulders — still no side rails, no bottom; Full is unchanged.
- **Accent Focus Ring no longer paints over the poster** — with the ring and No Zoom on together, the ring hugged the artwork so tightly it covered the edge; the art now insets inside the ring.
- **The hero commits its artwork once** — cold launches could repaint the hero as catalogs raced in; it now commits a single time, no more artwork swap after boot.
- **GIF covers: cadence over the last bit of sharpness** — collection focus GIFs keep every source frame (smoothness) at HD-parity resolution or a touch under, instead of dropping frames to chase pixels.
- **This Apple TV now shows up in your account's device list** — device registration had been rejected on every launch since the feature arrived because the app announced itself under a name the backend doesn't accept; it now registers cleanly (the platform still reads tvOS).
- Under the hood: settings toggles announce On/Off to VoiceOver; the Apple TV's collection edits now push to the account (previously pull-only); settings-sync observation re-arms after a sign-out → sign-in without a relaunch; a big test-suite expansion (server discovery, filter editor, reveal gate, and more). Localization: French, Spanish, German, Italian and Vietnamese all carry every new string.

### Answers to open questions
- **"Do changes on my Nuvio account propagate automatically?"** — Yes, without signing out: the Apple TV pulls everything (addons, plugins, library, watch progress, collections, home-screen catalog settings) when you pick your profile and every time the app comes back to the foreground. It does not poll while sitting open on Home — background it and re-open. Appearance preferences (theme, poster style, player settings) are per-platform by design, so phone/web changes to those don't reach the TV.
- **"Can the top pill bar be hidden / moved?" (FEAT-17/25)** — We're keeping the system tab bar: it's the native tvOS one, it already minimizes as you scroll into content (like Apple's own apps), and a single Menu press from deep in a page jumps you back to the top. Hiding or re-implementing it is the Android-TV-port feel this app exists to avoid — same reasoning as keeping the system focus motion.
- **BUG-38 (collection backdrops)** — still open on our side: if your configured collection backdrop isn't rendering, please export your Collections JSON from the phone app and share it in the thread — the payload is the answer.
- **BUG-31 (No Zoom on Focus everywhere)** — closed on your confirmation, thanks.
