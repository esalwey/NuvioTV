## Highlights

- **Simkl integration** — connect your Simkl account (Settings → Accounts & Services) with a short PIN code and NuvioTV keeps your watch history in sync: movies and episodes scrobble as you watch, anime resolves through Simkl's own ID mapping (with a picker when a match is ambiguous), and a **Sync Now** button pushes/pulls on demand.
- **Your provider keys follow your account** — debrid and TMDB API keys now sync through your Nuvio account, so signing in on another Apple TV brings your sources with it. This also fixes a silent failure where credential pushes from the Apple TV were being rejected by the backend — sync actually works now. (AllDebrid stays device-local for the moment.)
- **The See All crash is fixed** — the P0 from the beta thread: pressing Back out of an expanded search-results grid no longer ejects you to the tvOS home screen (reported on tvOS 27). See All grids also **remember your position** now — open a title deep in a grid, press Back, and you're on the card you left, not back at the top.
- **Focus zoom is whole-card again** — in beta.10 the zoom happened *inside* the artwork while the tile edge stood still, and the lifted artwork could swallow the title below it. The whole card now lifts as one piece and the caption stays visible. New companion option: **No Zoom on Focus** (Settings → Appearance) marks the focused card with a border and shadow instead of scaling, for a calmer shelf.
- **White-theme contrast sweep** — another pass over light-on-light surfaces: stream picker rows and group headers, cloud library, add-on rows, Collections tabs, and the accent focus ring's near-white fallback.
- **Collections polish** — folder tiles fall back to first-item artwork when a collection ships no cover, GIFs decode at tile size (smoother rows), collection titles render as logo artwork where available — and branded service covers no longer double-print their wordmark.
- Plus: the pinned hero commits its metadata once (no more raw-then-localized double swap), hero-off mode keeps the focus-follow info panel, trailer-pipeline hardening after source exhaustion, detail-page focus traps removed, and the UI test suite is up to 54 tests.
